ChangeRadar

v0.2 public

Score a Kubernetes or Helm change against past incidents. MiniLM embeddings, cosine ranking, a helm diff parser, and a public seed of 1,513 labeled cases. Bring your own incident JSONL. No training required. 

helm diff upgrade checkout ./chart -f values-prod.yaml \
  | python -m changeradar score-diff

Live Space

Runs MiniLM in the browser via Transformers.js. First load ~20MB, then local.

Public dataset

RCAEval, Cloud-OpsBench, AnoMod labels. Not production dumps.

Honest metric

Hit@k is lookalike ranking. Cosine is not P(outage).

A high score means “this looks like a known incident.” It does not mean the change will page in production.
